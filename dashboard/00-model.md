## The question

Which protocol that records no token is worth farming next, and what would its token be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is? The radar ranks 198 protocols: 40 on the snapshot's watchlist (at or above $50M of TVL), 20 more at or above the floor that the watchlist itself leaves off, and 138 below the floor. Early rows total 158. Every row records no token at all: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. The rule also drops versioned pools, the watchlist rule's named bridge and liquid-staking/restaking categories, names with wrapped/staked/staking, and CEX products; DefiLlama files some sub-products under other category strings, and those stay, labelled with their category. The watchlist is the floor and never the ceiling.

## The model: a comps-fitted log multiple

The snapshot's live-token protocols are the market's own answer to what a protocol's TVL is worth once a token trades. The fit sample is every live protocol with a market cap and TVL of at least $1M and no CEX category: **102 comps**. Regress

    y  = log10(mcap_usd / tvl_usd)                      winsorized at p1=-2.396, p99=1.556
    x1 = log10(tvl_usd)
    x2 = clamp(change_7d ?? 0, -60, 120) / 100
    x3 = log10(1 + (age_days ?? 365) / 365)
    x4 = min(chains.length, 8) / 8

by ordinary least squares through the normal equations and Gaussian elimination, then three Huber IRLS rounds (delta = 1.5 x residual MAD, weight min(1, delta/|r|)). The fitted coefficients, intercept 4.709:

| Feature | Coefficient |
| --- | --- |
| log10 TVL | -0.628 |
| 7d growth / 100 | -0.200 |
| log10(1 + age/365) | -0.619 |
| chains / 8 | -0.187 |

Each category's median residual (actual minus fitted) is shrunk by n/(n+8) and clamped to [-1, 1]: a partially pooled category effect that vanishes for a category with no comps. Then

    multiple          = clamp(10^(fitted + category adjustment), 0.02, 8)
    implied_value_usd = max(1, round(tvl_usd x multiple))

Rows rank by implied value, then TVL, then slug. The category adjustment is computed from the snapshot's own live protocols, never from a hard-coded table.

Five deterministic folds, one per `fnv1a(slug) % 5`; each fold refits on the other four (category effects included) and predicts the held-out comps. Out-of-fold:

| comps / folds | 102 / 5 |

| 80% interval coverage | 76.5% |

An MAE near 0.55 means the typical prediction is off by about 3.6x either way; the intervals are wide on purpose and say so. Each row's range comes from the out-of-fold residual q10/q90, widened by 1 + 1/sqrt(n + 1) for a category with n comps, so a thin category gets a visibly wider bar.

## Farm economics

Position size from TVL: $50 under $10M, $100 under $50M, $250 under $250M, else $500. Hold period from age: 60 days under 180 days or unknown, 45 under 365, else 30. Costs are illustrative estimates, stated on the page: gas $8 on Ethereum, $3 on Base/Arbitrum/Optimism/Polygon, $1 on Solana, $4 otherwise; bridge $2 when the protocol names more than one chain; opportunity cost at 5% a year for the hold. Out of pocket = gas + bridge + opportunity. Break-even airdrop % = total cost / position x 100, and net at 10%, 25% and 50% is position x rate minus that cost. Every row carries seven steps: wallet, funding, the category action, weekly activity, the hold, the claim check and the netting line that names the total cost, the break-even percentage and the three scenario nets.

## Limitations

The model is a cross-sectional comps fit, not a forecast of a launch: it says what a protocol with this record has been worth in the market, not whether or when a token arrives. The fit sample is 102 live protocols and the market's multiples are noisy, so R2 is modest; the category effect is shrunk hard and a category with one comp moves least. TVL is DefiLlama's own figure and can be recursive, double-counted or inflated by incentives; seven-day growth is temporary and the age feature only proxies maturity. The snapshot is frozen at 2026-11-14: rerun `main.ts` on a fresh snapshot to move the board. The costs are estimates for one position, not quotes, and the break-even ignores taxes and slippage. No row's implied value uses a protocol's own market cap; live protocols are never ranked.
